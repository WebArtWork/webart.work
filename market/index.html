Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a store and a marketplace?

A store usually sells one business’s products. A multi-vendor marketplace lets independent sellers operate within one platform and needs additional onboarding, permissions, moderation, commissions and settlement processes.

Can we start with one seller and expand later?

Yes, but future vendor ownership, roles, order routing and payment requirements should influence the initial architecture.

Can suppliers manage their own products?

Yes. We can provide seller dashboards and approval workflows with appropriate data isolation.

Can you build B2B pricing and wholesale orders?

Yes. Business accounts, negotiated prices, minimum order quantities, bulk orders, quotes and repeat purchasing can be included.

Can you integrate payments and shipping?

Yes, where the provider supports the required workflow, country, business type and API access. We assess this before implementation.

Can you connect to our CRM or ERP?

Yes. We review the available APIs, data mapping, synchronization requirements and operational rules.

Can you build a mobile marketplace app?

Yes. We can build connected iOS and Android apps when a dedicated mobile experience is justified.

Do you only develop with Angular?

No. Angular is a core expertise, but we select suitable frontend, backend or commerce technologies for each project.

How much does development cost, and how long does it take?

That depends on the business model, design, user roles, workflows, integrations and migration needs. After discovery, we provide a project-specific scope, estimate and timeline.

Do you provide maintenance and take over existing platforms?

Yes. We assess existing software before taking responsibility and can agree on upgrades, support and ongoing feature delivery.

Can you guarantee sales or SEO rankings?

No. We can implement solid technical foundations and measurement tools, but business results depend on products, pricing, competition and marketing.
