Our development foundation

IT Kamianets 3D Engine

One shared foundation for many games and 3D apps.

Instead of rebuilding the same systems for every project, we keep them in reusable Unity packages, shared JSON schemas and game constructors. Each game keeps its own gameplay, interface, assets and rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you develop a Unity game from scratch?

Yes: planning, prototype, gameplay, interfaces, environments, backend, testing and release support.

Do you develop 2D and 3D games?

Yes. The approach depends on the gameplay, visuals and target platforms.

Can you build multiplayer games?

Yes: sessions, synchronization, game servers and backend, with the architecture chosen for your game and scale.

Do you build VR apps for Meta Quest?

Yes. We check headset compatibility, supported interactions and performance for each project.

Can you build AR or mixed reality apps?

Yes. Available features depend on the hardware and SDK.

What is IT Kamianets 3D Engine?

Our modular ecosystem of Unity packages, JSON schemas and game constructors. It is in active development and serves as a base for compatible projects.

Does the 3D Engine replace Unity?

No. It is built on Unity. Its scene and asset formats are engine-independent, so other engines could be added later.

Will you use the framework in my project?

Only where its packages fit your requirements. Many projects also need custom Unity work or other approaches.

Will my game share code with other projects?

General functionality may come from shared packages; your mechanics, content and interface stay yours. Ownership, licensing and dependencies are set in the agreement.

Can you work on an existing Unity project?

Yes. We review the code, assets, dependencies and architecture first.

Can you help with 3D models and assets?

Yes: we prepare, adapt and integrate assets or coordinate their creation. Original modeling and animation are scoped separately.

Can you build a 3D product configurator?

Yes, including links to your catalog, pricing and inquiry workflow.

Can you build architectural walkthroughs?

Yes, from suitable models and plans, for desktop, mobile or VR.

Can a room scan become an interactive 3D scene?

Our framework already explores this with Meta Quest. Feasibility depends on the scan, hardware and accuracy needed, so we start with an assessment or prototype.

Do you build browser-based Unity apps?

Yes, when the features suit browser builds. VR in the browser needs extra validation.

Can the app connect to our existing software?

Yes: APIs, databases, sign-in, admin platforms and business software.

How much does Unity development cost?

It depends on gameplay, art, environments, platforms, multiplayer and integrations. A prototype is a very different investment from a full game. You get an estimate after we review your needs.

How long does a Unity project take?

It depends on complexity, assets and platforms. Larger projects are split into milestones, starting with a prototype or MVP.

Can you help launch the app?

Yes: builds, release setup and store submission. Platform approval is up to the platform operator.

Can you take over an unfinished Unity project?

Yes, after a technical review of what exists and the risks involved.
