Our approach: AI that fits your software

  • Start with the workflow, not the model

    We first establish who needs help, what they need to accomplish, which systems hold the relevant information and what success looks like.

  • Use the right combination

    A practical solution can combine standard business logic, search, API integrations, AI models and human approvals. Not every task needs an autonomous agent.

  • Choose technology for your requirements

    We can integrate services from providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic and Google, or evaluate locally hosted models, selected per project rather than one exclusive partnership.

  • Keep costs visible

    We can track model usage, estimate processing costs, establish application-side budgets, and design caching, batching or simpler alternatives where suitable.

  • Build for real operations

    We consider permissions, versioning, logging, errors, fallback behavior and maintainability, not only a successful demonstration.

Security, privacy & human control

Business AI may interact with internal documents, customer information and operational systems. We design safeguards according to the data and risk involved.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know which AI model to use?

No. Explain the problem, users, available information and intended result. We can recommend a suitable combination of models, standard software and automation.

Can you integrate AI into our existing CRM or website?

Yes, where the system's architecture and permissions allow it. We assess APIs, data access, security and the workflow before development.

What is the difference between an AI chatbot and an AI agent?

A chatbot primarily supports conversation and answers. An agent may also use approved tools to complete defined tasks. Tool access, boundaries and human approvals are part of the design.

Can the solution use our private documents?

Possibly. We first assess ownership, permissions, data sensitivity, provider terms and an appropriate retrieval or hosting architecture. Private information is not automatically safe simply because it is used with AI.

Can we use our own API keys or AI provider accounts?

Yes, when supported by the selected provider and project architecture. Credential ownership, security, billing responsibilities and access boundaries are agreed during planning.

How much does an AI project cost?

Development cost depends on the workflow, complexity, data preparation, integrations, evaluation and support requirements. Model usage and third-party infrastructure can create separate recurring charges. We provide an estimate after discovery.

Will AI always be accurate or save us money?

No. Quality, latency, running costs and human review vary by use case. We recommend pilots and benchmarks before making business-critical commitments.

Can you maintain and improve the system after launch?

Yes. We can support monitoring, updates, quality evaluations, integrations and new features under an agreed maintenance scope.
