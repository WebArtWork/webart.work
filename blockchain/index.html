Where blockchain helps a business

verified

Document verification

Store a document hash on the chain while the document stays in secure storage, so you can later prove it has not changed.

Example: A business app records proof of an approved document for later checks.

This proves integrity, not that the content is true or legally valid.

sports_esports

Games and marketplaces

Transferable assets, user-owned wallets and shared economies, built together with the games and accounts around them.

Example: Several games share one currency for rewards, purchases and trades.

loyalty

Loyalty and membership

Token-based rewards and memberships shared across connected products and your existing software.

Example: Customers earn rewards they can spend in several connected products.

handshake

Multi-company networks

A common record when independent organizations must share and verify transactions.

Example: Partners exchange transaction updates under agreed validation rules.

We check whether this beats a conventional integration first.

account_balance

Financial applications

Programmable workflows, digital asset management and settlement records.

Example: Smart contracts execute agreed conditions and record settlement.

Security, custody and regulatory reviews depend on the project and jurisdiction.

Do you need your own blockchain?

We usually compare three options.

public

Use an existing blockchain

Right for most tokens, marketplaces, smart contracts and Web3 apps. You get its ecosystem and work within its limits.

deployed_code

Build a custom blockchain

When you need protocol logic, governance or permissions no network offers. More control, much more work to run.

storage

Use conventional software

Without shared verification, on-chain assets or decentralized coordination, a backend and database is simpler and cheaper.

We recommend what fits your requirements, not the option with the most blockchain in it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does my company need blockchain technology?

Not necessarily. It matters when you need shared verifiable records, digital assets, programmable transactions or decentralized coordination. We assess this before recommending it.

Can you build a complete blockchain application?

Yes: smart contracts, interfaces, backend, APIs, wallet integrations and infrastructure. The exact scope depends on the project.

Can you create a custom cryptocurrency or token?

We build the technical side: issuance, transfers, distribution and app integration. Token economics, legal classification and regulation need separate consideration.

Can you build our own blockchain?

Yes, with Cosmos SDK. First we check whether your own network is justified or an existing blockchain fits better.

Which blockchain technologies do you support?

Cosmos SDK and EVM smart contracts, plus the wallets, APIs and infrastructure around them. Specific networks and versions are chosen during planning.

Can you add blockchain to our existing application?

Yes. We integrate wallets, tokens, transaction verification or smart contracts into websites, mobile apps, CRMs and marketplaces.

How secure are smart contracts?

Contracts can contain vulnerabilities and transactions may be irreversible. We use testing, review and security controls, and projects holding significant value may need an independent audit.

Who owns the software you develop?

Ownership, source code, infrastructure accounts and handover are set in the project agreement during planning.

How much does blockchain development cost?

It depends on scope. A contract integration, a dApp and your own network are very different efforts. After discovery we send a scope, milestones and estimate.

How long does development take?

It depends on features, integrations, security needs and deployment. A prototype is much smaller than a full platform or network launch. You get a timeline during planning.

Can you maintain our existing blockchain project?

Yes. We start with a technical review of your contracts, apps and infrastructure, then agree the support scope.

Can blockchain replace our database?

Usually not. Most blockchain apps still use a database for private data, search and reporting. We use each technology for what it does well.
