Could quantum computing help your business?

Some problems have thousands or millions of possible combinations. Quantum computing explores new ways to solve some of them. The technology is still developing, but you can already test it on your problem.

It may be worth testing if your problem has:

Illustrative scenario

Example: smarter housekeeping scheduling

A hotel has dozens of rooms, several housekeepers, different cleaning times and arrival deadlines. Work has to be shared efficiently while respecting staff availability and when each room must be ready.

The result

A working scheduling experiment, measured comparisons and a clear recommendation. It may find a promising quantum approach or confirm that a classical algorithm is better for production. Either answer helps the hotel decide.

Our approach

  1. 1Define rooms, staff, deadlines and constraints.
  2. 2Build a conventional optimization model.
  3. 3Build a simplified quantum optimization experiment.
  4. 4Run simulations, and real hardware when justified.
  5. 5Compare quality, time, feasibility and cost.
  6. 6Recommend the best approach for daily operations.

Honest about the current limits

Today's quantum processors are noisy, limited and costly to run. For most everyday business problems, classical algorithms remain the established and cheaper option, and quantum experiments guarantee no gain in speed, cost or quality.

That is why every project starts with a feasibility check, uses measurable benchmarks and keeps a classical approach in the comparison.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to understand quantum computing?

No. Explain your business problem or software idea, and we will assess whether quantum computing is relevant.

Can quantum computing improve my existing software?

Possibly, depending on the problem. We first find suitable tasks and test whether a quantum approach helps. Many applications are better served by conventional algorithms.

Do I need an IBM Quantum account?

Not for simulation or classical development. Real IBM Quantum hardware needs IBM credentials, and we help you set them up.

Who pays for IBM Quantum hardware?

IBM bills hardware usage to the IBM account the job runs on. Web Art Work's assessment, development and support are scoped and billed separately.

Can you add quantum features to our CRM or application?

Yes. We build API integrations and workflows for your existing application, or a new application around the feature.

Is our business data secure?

Sensitive data is not sent to quantum providers. Experiments use an abstract mathematical model of the problem, and data handling is reviewed before we design the integration.

How long does a quantum project take?

It depends on the problem and whether you need an assessment, a prototype or an integration. After the first call we send a scope and delivery plan.

What if quantum computing is not the right solution?

We explain why and recommend an alternative. We can continue with classical optimization or conventional development.
