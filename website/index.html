Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to build a website?

Our packages start at $200. The final price depends on design, pages, features, integrations and content. Projects outside the packages get an individual estimate.

How long does it take to build a website?

A site on a ready structure can launch within a few days once materials are ready. Custom and Premium packages usually take one to three weeks. Larger projects are planned individually.

Can you create a completely custom design?

Yes. We can design from scratch around your brand, work from your existing designs or adapt a layout when that is more economical.

Do you only build websites with Angular?

No. Angular is one of our main tools, but we also use React, Next.js, Vue and others, depending on the project.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We review the current site first, then improve design, navigation, speed or features, or rebuild parts of it.

Can you build an online store?

Yes: catalog, product management, cart, checkout, payments and integrations, designed around your products and systems.

Can you build a website in several languages?

Yes, with language switching, localized content and the right technical SEO. Languages and scope are agreed during planning.

Can I edit the content myself?

Yes, when content management is part of the solution. We can build an editor, connect a CMS or update content for you.

Will my website appear on Google?

We build technical SEO foundations and offer extended SEO services. Specific rankings and traffic cannot be guaranteed.

Can you connect the site to my CRM or other software?

Yes: CRMs, payment gateways, booking platforms and other tools, depending on their APIs.

Can you help with texts and images?

Yes. We help with structure, copywriting and visuals. The amount of content work is set in the package or agreed separately.

Who provides hosting and the domain?

You can use your own, or we set them up for you. Hosting, domain and third-party costs are clarified during planning.

Will I own my website?

Ownership, source code, design files, domain access and handover are defined in the agreement, so responsibilities are clear.

Can you add features later?

Yes: new pages, features, languages and integrations, up to connected web applications and custom backends.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance?

Yes: updates, fixes, support, content changes, speed improvements and new development, as agreed.

Can you take over a site built by another company?

Yes. We first review the code, infrastructure and dependencies to find risks and plan maintenance or migration.
