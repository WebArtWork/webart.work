Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between CRM and BOS?

CRM focuses on customer relationships and sales. A Business Operating System connects a wider set of company operations, which may include CRM, scheduling, staff, jobs, invoices and reporting.

Did WAW build ArboStar?

Members of our team contributed for approximately seven years. ArboStar is an independent platform involving multiple contributors; we do not claim to have built or to own it entirely.

Can you build something similar to ArboStar?

We can design a new industry-specific system with independently developed and appropriately scoped CRM, estimating, scheduling, field operations, invoicing and reporting functionality.

Can you build a CRM from scratch or improve an existing one?

Yes. We offer greenfield development, MVPs, feature extensions, modernization, integration and maintenance after an initial technical review.

Can the CRM support multiple branches, companies or customers?

Yes. We can plan role permissions, organization hierarchies and multi-tenant or multi-company isolation around your business model.

Can employees use it on mobile or offline?

Yes. Supported mobile platforms and selected offline workflows can be included when the project requires them.

Can you connect websites, accounting, payments and third-party tools?

Yes, subject to available APIs, permissions and provider requirements. Migration from an existing system is also possible after data assessment.

Can you add AI to the CRM?

Yes, for suitable tasks such as search, summarization, classification, document processing and assistance, with safeguards and cost review.

How much will it cost and how long will it take?

It depends on modules, roles, business rules, integrations, data migration, design and security. We define phased estimates and milestones after discovery.

Who owns the resulting software and can WAW maintain it?

Source-code ownership, IP, infrastructure accounts, licensing and handover are specified in the contract. Ongoing maintenance and development can be provided separately.
