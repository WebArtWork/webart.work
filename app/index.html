Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to develop a mobile app?

It depends on features, design, platforms, backend and integrations. A small MVP and a large marketplace are very different. After we discuss your needs, you get a scope and estimate.

How long does it take to build an app?

It depends on complexity, requirements and integrations. We estimate during planning and split the work into milestones.

Can one app work on iOS and Android?

Yes. Cross-platform technology shares much of the app between both. How much depends on the features and architecture.

Do you only use Angular and Capacitor?

No. They are our core tools, but we also use other frameworks and native development when the product needs it.

Should I build a hybrid or a native app?

Cross-platform suits most business and consumer apps. Native is better for specialized device features, demanding performance or platform-specific behavior. We compare both first.

Can you build the backend too?

Yes: APIs, databases, authentication, business logic and infrastructure, or integration with your existing backend.

Can my app work without internet?

Selected workflows can work offline, storing data on the device and syncing when the connection returns.

Can you add payments and subscriptions?

Yes. The right method depends on your business model, platforms and store policies.

Can you add AI features?

Yes: assistants, recommendations, content processing and search. We check reliability, privacy and running costs first.

Can you publish my app to the App Store and Google Play?

Yes. We help with accounts, signing, listings and submission. Apple and Google make the final decision and may ask for changes.

Who owns the developer accounts?

We recommend you keep your own Apple and Google accounts and give us delegated access for publishing.

Who owns the source code?

Code ownership, IP rights, infrastructure access and handover are set in the agreement before development starts.

Can you turn my website into an app?

Possibly. We review the site first. Some parts can be reused, but simply wrapping a website rarely makes a good store app.

Can you improve or finish an existing app?

Yes. We review the code, dependencies, documentation and risks, then propose fixes, modernization or next steps.

Can you build an admin dashboard?

Yes, for users, products, payments, content and analytics. It can run in the browser even when the product is a mobile app.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance?

Yes: fixes, OS updates, security, new features and releases, with the scope agreed separately.

Will my app be secure?

We plan security around your features and risks. Testing, data handling and any external audits are set in the scope. No software is guaranteed free of vulnerabilities.

What if Apple or Google rejects the app?

We review the feedback, find the rule involved and help make the changes.

Can we start with an MVP and grow later?

Yes, and we often recommend it. You validate the product first and plan the next features with real user data.
